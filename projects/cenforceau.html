01 · SILO & CATEGORY OPTIMIZATION

Connect topic discovery to relevant product categories

SEO audit · keyword and blog plans

The website audit flagged silo structure as an area to fix. I used keyword grouping and page mapping to clarify which topics belonged on category pages and which should support them as editorial content.

01 · AUDIT

Find the structure gaps

Reviewed indexation, technical and on-page SEO, mobile experience, content and site architecture to identify where category paths needed clearer organization.

02 · ORGANIZE

Rework category silos

Group pages by search intent, align keyword clusters to category and supporting pages, and make internal paths between information content and relevant product pages clearer.

03 · CONNECT

Map keywords and links

Build the content map, allocate primary and supporting keywords to target pages, then plan internal links that help users move between topics and product categories.
