CASE STUDY

Project Background & Execution

01 / BEFORE THE MERGE

Two Domains

FoxScore previously operated across m.foxscore.com and foxscore.com.

02 / DURING THE MERGE

Migration Signals Were Missed

The domains were consolidated and the sitemap rebuilt, but redirects, canonical tags, SSR output and internal 404 links were not handled consistently.

03 / INDEXING IMPACT

Major Loss of Indexed Pages

A Google Search Console snapshot last updated 9 Nov 2026 showed 6 indexed and 827 not indexed pages.

04 / CURRENT STATUS

Recovery Requires Validation

The migration issues need to be audited and validated across redirects, canonicals, server-rendered pages and broken links, then indexing progress monitored in Search Console.

MIGRATION RECOVERY

Recovery Priorities

01 / REDIRECTS

Map Old URLs to Their New Destinations

Match URLs from m.foxscore.com to the corresponding foxscore.com pages and validate permanent redirects.

02 / CANONICALS

Align Canonical Signals

Check that canonical tags point to the preferred live URL and agree with redirects and sitemap entries.

03 / SSR

Validate Rendered Pages

Confirm that important page content, metadata and crawlable links are present in the server-rendered and rendered HTML.

04 / 404s & SITEMAP

Repair Links and Resubmit Clean URLs

Fix internal links to 404 pages, then check that the sitemap lists only preferred, indexable URLs that return successfully.

These are validation priorities; completion and index recovery have not yet been verified.
